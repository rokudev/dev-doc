App analytics reports show how viewers use your app and how well the app performs on Roku devices. Roku generates these reports automatically for your apps. To learn how to open, filter, export, and schedule them, see [Analytics and sales reports for apps](doc:analytics).

* [App Engagement](doc:channel-engagement): Installs, visits, and streaming data for all apps.
* [App Health](doc:channel-health): BrightScript crashes and buffering data for all apps.
* [App Stability](doc:channel-stability): BrightScript crashes and memory closures for all apps across all Roku device models.
* [Viewership Summary](doc:viewership-summary): A small selection of app engagement and app health data.

If you distribute content on The Roku Channel, see [Roku Content Partner Portal analytics](doc:roku-content-partner-portal-analytics).
