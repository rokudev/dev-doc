The Roku Launchpad includes an email preferences center that lets you choose which types of Roku Developer Program emails to receive. You can select one or more of the following emails or unsubscribe from all of them:

* Quarterly newsletters
* Events (webinars, Developer Summit)
* Platform updates (OS releases, developer tool updates, beta program announcements)
* Business updates (tax and billing updates, monetization policy, regulatory compliance)

To set your email preferences, [go to the Roku Launchpad](https://developer.roku.com/dev/landing) and click **Email Preferences**. You can also click the **Email Preferences** link in the footer of any Roku Developer's email. You then select the checkboxes for the notifications you want to receive and click **Save preferences**.

![](https://files.readme.io/b0674f5aaf3b135a29dd16aba2c5c478857306a43f359991f01377ce3bbb0816-image.png)

> Regardless of your selections, you will always receive crucial service notifications, such as updates to the certification criteria or changes to the Roku Distribution Agreement.
