Publishers and agencies can use Roku Ads Manager ([https://ads.roku.com](https://ads.roku.com)) to programmatically purchase in-stream CTV video ads in order to promote apps, increase viewership, and reach users on the Roku platform. This single tool enables the targeting of viewers with Roku's exclusive audience data, launching ad campaigns to convert viewers, and then measuring and optimizing campaign performance in real-time.
