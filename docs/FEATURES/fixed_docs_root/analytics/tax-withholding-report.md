# Tax Withholding Report

The Tax Withholding Report lists the monthly payouts you have received from Roku from the sales of subscription and one-time purchase products in your app. This report breaks down the gross revenue generated by the app, Roku's revenue share, tax withheld, currency conversion rate , and the net payable amount. This helps you understand how your net payouts are calculated after Roku's revenue share, tax withholding, and currency conversion are applied to your app's gross revenue.
