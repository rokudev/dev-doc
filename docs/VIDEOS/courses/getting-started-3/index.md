The getting started videos introduce developers to the Roku platform. They explain how streaming  works and the benefits of developing streaming applications for the Roku platform. Before starting any Roku course, developers should watch these videos.  

This getting started section includes the following videos:

- **[Streaming overview](doc:streaming-overview)**: This video summarizes how streaming works on the Roku platform. It explains how content is delivered from a content delivery network (CDN) to a Roku device via a content feed.



- [**Creating an SVOD app: First steps**](doc:svod-first-steps): This video explains the first steps for monetizing content on the Roku streaming platform with a subscription app. It is intended for publishers and content owners who are new to Roku and are ready to distribute their content to Roku's global audience of over 50 million active user accounts.



- **[Roku development overview](doc:roku-overview)**: This video explains why Roku's rapidly growing user base makes developing for the Roku platform a viable opportunity. More and more publishers and video content creators around the globe are looking to expand their reach and monetize their content with Roku, which means there's an increasing demand for qualified Roku developers.



- **[Enabling Developer Mode](doc:developer-mode)**: This video demonstrates how to enable Developer Mode on your Roku device so you can sideload and test your development channels.
