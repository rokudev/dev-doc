# Roku Advertising Watermark Webinar

In July 2022, Roku explained the Roku Advertising Watermark to developers. Topics covered in this webinar included:

- What is the Roku Advertising Watermark.
- How does the watermark help publishers, advertisers, and partners.
- Requirements for implementing the watermark.
- How developers can integrate the watermark.
