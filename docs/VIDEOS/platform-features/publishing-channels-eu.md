## About this video

This video explains how developers can surface their Roku apps to relevant viewers in new Streaming Store regions in Europe as soon as they become available. It describes how to update your app properties from within the [Developer Dashboard](https://developer.roku.com/developer), and it explains how to update search feeds to provide content ratings from the applicable content rating sources for apps participating in Roku Search. For apps offering subscriptions, the video concludes by describing how to verify the pricing of in-app products in Euros and provide localized product names.
